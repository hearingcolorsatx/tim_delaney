Tim Delaney

Artist | Producer | Musical Poet

Tim Delaney is a Marine Corps veteran, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist from Austintonio, Texas whose music blends raw acoustic pop, psychedelic folk textures, and harmony-rich songwriting. His debut EP Where to Begin showcases intimate acoustic arrangements layered with lush vocal harmonies and subtle psychedelic textures driven by the steady, unique combination of percussive shakers and noise makers, the result of which musically and lyrically illustrates what can only be described as the Theology of Temptation. Delaney releases music through his artist-owned label Hearing Colors LLC, where he also currently designs and maintains the label’s digital presence.
